\begin{abstract}
	\hspace*{\parindent}In the current scenario, Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) councils of Naga City adopt manual and semi-digital systems to record activities for youth programs, such as participants' data, cost, and much more relevant information. Such an approach led to insufficient documentation, delayed reporting, ineffective monitoring, and transparency. Therefore, the proposed project introduces a multi-tenant web-based information system for youth named KABISIG (Kabataang Bagong Sistema para sa Inklusibong Gobyerno).\vspace{5mm} 
    
    \hspace*{\parindent}KABISIG is able to record all the data of youth members and also automate registration and attendance with real-time analysis. Furthermore, it helps to improve the program by adding new features such as civic feedback with an anonymity option (\textit{Boses ng Kabataan}), resolution tracking, youth polling, and cross-barangay federation analytics, among others. The platform is built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and TailwindCSS on the frontend, and an Express (Node.js) API with Supabase (PostgreSQL) on the backend. It also implements a web-based three-tier architecture along with Agile Scrum methodology. There are five user types across seven functional actors: Super Administrator (SK Federation President), SK Chairperson (Barangay Administrator), SK Officials (Kagawad, Secretary, and Treasurer), Youth Constituent, and Public Viewer.\vspace{5mm}
    
    \hspace*{\parindent}The solution provided by the platform allows different Sangguniang Kabataan councils of Naga City to have a multi-tenant facility. As a result, it provides them with the infrastructure to maintain their records separately. Such a solution helps them to gain benefits individually and implement e-governance in the field of youth management at the barangay level.\vspace{5mm}
\end{abstract}
